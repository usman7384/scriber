# M5 demo parts, benchmarks and timing (build agent)

Rows: DP-1, DP-3, DP-4, DP-6, DP-8, DP-11, DP-12, DP-13, DP-14, DP-15, DP-16, DP-18, DP-19, DP-20, DP-22, GE-8, GE-11, VM-8, PF-1, PF-3, PF-7, PF-8, WP-15. None closes on this branch: every row needs the live run (D-030), which was not possible while the GPU was training.

## What landed

- `evals/cases/parts.yaml`: 50 cases for the demo-part rows (DP-14 and DP-15 have 16 each, DP-18 has 6 spec documents, DP-20 has 3 fixtures). `evals/cases/benchmark.yaml`: 30 spec-driven parts for GE-8 and VM-8. Both are in our own wording, with no 8-gram shared with the m1, dev, challenge, synthetic or grounded prompts. Both files are listed in `EVAL_CASES` in the training leak guard. That gives 80 new eval specs; M5 asks for 50 or more.
- `evals/parity/m5/reference/`: 30 hand-built parametric reference designs, one per benchmark case, generated by `make_reference.py`. Each builds as one valid solid and passes every check of its case with the geometry worker (VM-8 checks this again on every run). 21 of the 30 also meet the API's default DFM rules. The other 9 put holes closer to the edge than 1.5 × Ø, because their specs say so.
- `evals/parity/m5/vision/`: 8 dimensioned drawings (4 plates, 4 flanges) with `truth.json` (48 parameters) for GE-11, rendered by `make_fixtures.py`.
- `evals/src/partwright_evals/parity_m5_parts.py` (`ROWS`; not yet in `parity.registry()`, so the integrator needs to add it there).

## Gaps

- No live numbers yet; every row stays in progress until the integrator's run.
- PF-1, PF-3, PF-7 and PF-8 measure this workstation, not the reference laptop of `docs/HARDWARE.md`. WP-15 guards sockets in-process. Ollama and the geometry worker run as local processes, outside that guard.
- GE-11 uses drawings only; IN-5 covers the photo case.
- DP-8 assumes the hex collar is wider than the fitting. DP-11 assumes the lid's lip, or an inset lid, sits inside the box walls, so a lid that only rests on the rim has no clearance to measure. DP-19 measures gaps on probe lines through the bounding-box centre.
- DP-14 and DP-15 check variant identity by hole counts, axes, spacings and gusset counts. They do not check the shape names.
- The 2-link arm and PCB-case fixtures of DP-20 have no offline design. Only the jig is exercised offline.
