## Positioning notes

- **No offline, open, parametric option exists for engineering parts.** Zoo is open on the client side but needs its cloud engine. CADAM is GPL and needs a cloud LLM. Meshy and Tripo are not parametric. That gap is our wedge.
- **Spec- and rule-grounding (Kyrall's Context Manager) is the differentiator to match.** None of the open tools ingest PDFs or standards. Our knowledge graph plus machine-checkable rules (see `research/doc-parsing.md` and `research/knowledge-graph.md`) is the counterpart.
- **Assemblies with joints** are rare. Only Kyrall claims them, plus the host-CAD copilots that inherit them from Fusion/Onshape.
- **Feature-tree export** is the main thing users want to hand off. Copilots win here because they write native features. Our answer is the IR plus FreeCAD `.FCStd` replay (see `research/cad-backends.md`).
- **Licence hygiene:** CADAM is GPL-3.0, so do not copy its code into an Apache-2.0 project. Ideas only.
