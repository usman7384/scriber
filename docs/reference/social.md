### S40 Launch film (auto-captions + frames)
Narration (paraphrased): a working drone's frame where every part was designed by AI and can be manufactured; no human designed it. Mechanical design is manual, one complicated part can take a week. You describe what you need and it designs the model; the more context (spec sheet, past parts, design process) the more it designs "your way"; these become a knowledge graph used to design the part, embedding design intent and best practices in the assembly. Frame, control surfaces, mounts are designed until there is a whole drone to assemble. **If the centre of gravity is too far back, you tell it to move the battery mount and it regenerates the part on the spot.** Also robot arms, enclosures, brackets, jigs for robotics, automotive, aerospace. "Live today."
