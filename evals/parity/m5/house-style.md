# House style HS-1 (fixture company profile for CX-13)

Invented for the conventions fine-tune; not any real company's standard. A request that names a value overrides the profile.

| id | Convention |
|---|---|
| wall | Enclosures, trays and housings: 2.5 mm walls and floor. |
| insert | Screw points with no size given take M3 heat-set inserts: Ø4.0 mm bore, 6 mm deep. On enclosures they sit in Ø13 mm corner bosses from the floor to the rim. |
| fillet | 1 mm fillet on every vertical outside edge. |
| edge | Hole centres at least 2 × D from any outside edge (8 mm for an insert bore). |
| names | Parameter names are snake_case and end in their unit: `_mm`, `_deg`, or `_n` for counts. |
